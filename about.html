About Pink Mango Publishing

We publish practical, honest books for women navigating health conditions, hormonal changes, and neurodivergence. Our authors write from lived experience — not as distant experts, but as people who have been where you are.

Every book in our catalog is structured as a toolkit: grounded in research, filtered through real experience, and designed to be useful today — not someday.

Our Authors

Clara Bennett writes about ADHD, AuDHD, RSD, and neurodivergent women — from executive dysfunction to perimenopause to motherhood.

Elena Voss, MSc writes about bipolar, PMDD, CPTSD, anxiety, autism, and Saudi Arabian history.

James Hartley writes about AuDHD in men — a perspective that's almost entirely missing from the conversation.

Marcus Hale writes about technology, industrial design, and the future of manufacturing.

Sarah Steele, CPT writes about strength training for women over 50.

Hannah Wells writes about medical-adjacent women's health: GLP-1 medication, thyroid conditions, iron deficiency, migraine, PCOS, PMDD, diastasis recti, and sleep.
