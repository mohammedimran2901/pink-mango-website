# THE IRON FIX

**A Woman's Guide to Understanding Iron Deficiency, Reclaiming Your Energy, and Feeling Like Yourself Again**

**Author:** Hannah Wells

## Blurb (retail-ready)

You've been told you're tired because you're busy. You've been told to sleep more, stress less, try magnesium, cut back caffeine. But the exhaustion that lives in your bones — the brain fog, the hair falling out in the shower, the restless legs at night, the ice cravings, the feeling that you're running on empty no matter how much rest you get — is not a lifestyle problem. It could be iron deficiency, the most common and most overlooked nutritional condition in women's health. One in three premenopausal women has low iron stores, and most have no idea. Written by a health writer who spent years undiagnosed with a ferritin of 12, The Iron Fix is the practical, judgment-free guide to understanding what iron actually does, getting the right tests and interpreting them honestly, supplementing without destroying your digestive system, eating to build your stores, managing heavy bleeding, navigating pregnancy and postpartum, and finally reclaiming the energy you thought you'd lost forever. Every chapter ends with a toolkit you can use immediately.
