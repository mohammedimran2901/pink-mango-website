## Description
The AuDHD overwhelm paradox: autism craves quiet and order, ADHD generates chaos and noise. This book covers the four types of overwhelm (sensory, cognitive, emotional, social), the masking tax, the overwhelm cycle, and builds your personal overwhelm map and calm kit. Book 1 of the AuDHD Fix trilogy.
