# THE RESTLESS SOUL

**A Woman's Guide to ADHD, Spirituality, and Finding Peace in a Busy Brain**

**Author:** Clara Bennett

## Blurb

If you have an ADHD brain and have ever tried to meditate, pray, or sit still in spiritual practice — and felt like a failure — this book is for you. The Restless Soul bridges the gap between the neurodivergent mind and the spiritual life, offering a radically different approach: instead of trying to fix your brain to fit spiritual advice, it reimagines spiritual practice for the brain you actually have. From RSD and the Divine to hyperfocus as sacred state, from body-based grounding to micro-rituals that actually stick, this book validates the unique spiritual gifts of the ADHD brain while releasing the shame of the practices that never worked.
