## Description
The first book dedicated to the intersection of ADHD and perimenopause. Explains how estrogen decline affects dopamine regulation, why medication stops working, and provides practical strategies for HRT, medication adjustments, emotional regulation, sleep, and rebuilding your toolkit for this life stage.
