## Description
AuDHD burnout is autistic burnout (cumulative masking and sensory demands) combined with ADHD burnout (pushing through executive dysfunction). This book explains the four phases, shutdown, and the two-phase recovery protocol — radical rest, then rebuild. Book 2 of the AuDHD Fix trilogy.
