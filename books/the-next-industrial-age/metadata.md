## Description
A strategic examination of how industrial design will shape the future of Western legacy industries — aerospace, automotive, heavy machinery, chemicals, and energy. Covers digital twins, sustainability, materials innovation, human-centered automation, reshoring, and design-led reinvention through detailed case studies.
