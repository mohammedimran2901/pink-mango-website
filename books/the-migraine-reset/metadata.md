# THE MIGRAINE RESET

**A Woman's Guide to Understanding Triggers, Breaking the Cycle, and Getting Your Life Back**

**Author:** Hannah Wells

## Blurb (retail-ready)

Migraine is the second leading cause of disability in women under fifty worldwide — and yet most women spend years being told it's "just headaches." It is not. It is a complex neurological condition that affects your entire nervous system, and it deserves serious, specific treatment. Written by a health writer who has lived with chronic migraine for over a decade, The Migraine Reset is the practical guide to understanding what migraine actually is, navigating the hormonal connections that make women three times more likely to be affected, identifying your unique triggers through a threshold-based approach, mastering acute attacks with the right medications at the right time, exploring the new CGRP drugs that have changed the landscape, and building a long-term management plan that actually fits your life. Every chapter ends with a toolkit you can use immediately.
