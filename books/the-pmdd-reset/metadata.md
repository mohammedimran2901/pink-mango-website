# THE PMDD RESET

**A Woman's Guide to Understanding Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder, Breaking the Cycle, and Finding Relief**

**Author:** Hannah Wells

## Blurb (retail-ready)

PMDD affects an estimated 5 to 8 percent of menstruating women, yet most have never heard of it until they find themselves (or their daughter, or their partner) trapped in a monthly cycle of severe anxiety, rage, depression, and physical symptoms that arrive like clockwork two weeks before every period. It is not PMS. It is a severe biological condition — a brain that is exquisitely sensitive to normal hormonal fluctuations — and it is treatable. Written by a health writer who has lived with PMDD, The PMDD Reset is the practical guide to understanding what PMDD actually is, tracking your symptoms for diagnosis, the SSRI intermittent dosing that works within hours, hormonal treatments, nutrition and lifestyle strategies, how to navigate relationships and work when half your month is invisible, and how to build a long-term plan that gives you your life back. Every chapter ends with a toolkit you can use immediately.
