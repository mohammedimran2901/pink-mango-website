## Blurb (retail-ready)

You've been told your labs are normal. You've been told it's stress. You've been told to try yoga. But your body is telling you something different — exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix, brain fog that makes you forget words mid-sentence, weight that won't move no matter what you do, hair falling out in the shower, and a creeping sense that something is deeply wrong, even though every test says you're fine. You're not crazy. You're not lazy. You may be one of the fourteen million American women living with undiagnosed Hashimoto's thyroiditis — the most common autoimmune disease you've never heard of. Written by a health writer who spent years navigating her own Hashimoto's diagnosis, *The Unstoppable Thyroid* is the practical, judgment-free guide to understanding what's happening inside your body, getting the right diagnosis, navigating medication, managing fatigue and brain fog, eating to reduce inflammation without becoming obsessive, and finally feeling like yourself again. Every chapter ends with a toolkit you can use immediately. This is not medical advice — it is the resource you deserve.
