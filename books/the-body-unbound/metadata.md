# THE BODY UNBOUND

**A Woman's Guide to Understanding Endometriosis, Calming Inflammation, and Living Beyond the Pain**

**Author:** Hannah Wells

## Blurb

Endometriosis affects one in ten women, yet it remains one of the most misunderstood conditions in women's health. The Body Unbound is the practical guide that cuts through the confusion — explaining what endometriosis actually is (it's not just "bad periods"), how to get a diagnosis that's been delayed for years, what treatment options really work (with honest pros and cons), and how to build a full life around this condition. Written by a health writer who has been through it, this is the book that tells you what your doctor didn't have time to.
