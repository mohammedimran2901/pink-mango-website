# THE CORE FIX

**A Woman's Guide to Healing Diastasis Recti, Rebuilding Your Core, and Getting Strong After Baby**

**Author:** Hannah Wells

## Blurb (retail-ready)

Diastasis recti — the separation of the abdominal muscles along the midline — affects up to 60 percent of postpartum women, yet almost nobody is told about it. The standard advice ("do your Kegels, start doing crunches again when you're ready") is not just unhelpful — it actively widens the gap. Written by a health writer who healed her own diastasis recti, The Core Fix is the practical guide to understanding what diastasis is, checking yourself at home, the healing timeline (it takes months, not weeks), the breath and posture foundations, safe core exercises that actually close the gap, what exercises to avoid, the critical pelvic floor connection, how to move in daily life without making it worse, and when surgery is the right option. Every chapter ends with a toolkit you can use immediately.
