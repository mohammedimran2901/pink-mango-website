# STRONGER THAN YOU THINK

**The 50+ Woman's Guide to Strength Training for Muscle, Bone Health, and the Confidence You Thought You'd Lost**

**Author:** Sarah Steele, CPT

## Blurb (retail-ready)

You've been told to slow down, act your age, and leave the heavy lifting to someone else. Here's the truth: your body is still listening — and muscle, bone, and confidence all respond to load at any age. Written by a certified personal trainer who started lifting at forty-nine after a bone-density scare, *Stronger Than You Think* is the practical, judgment-free plan for women over fifty who want to rebuild their strength without crash diets, shame, or a gym full of fear. Inside you'll find a simple three-day lifting program, straight talk on bone health and training through menopause, protein-first eating that fits real plates, and the mental game of showing up and claiming your space. Six thousand American women turn fifty every day. This is the book for every one of them who wants to be stronger next year than she is today.
